# IndiHunt

A community-driven, full-stack platform for discovering, sharing, and discussing the best new software products built by Indian makers.

## Site Overview
- Users submit product launches of their own or upvote and review other launches they use and care to share with the community.
- Launches compete to be at the top of the leaderboard on the daily feed.
- Products launch daily with continuous voting, reviews, discussions, and developer Q&A.
- Homepage rank is driven by active feed tabs (Featured, Trending, All) and filters (Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month).
- Includes dynamic features like Hexagon Badges for user profiles (Tastemaker, Gone streaking).
- Built-in content moderation checking for text submissions.

## Product Discovery and Research
- **Products**:
  - Path format: `/products/{slug}`
  - Includes product details: name, tagline, description, launch date, makers, co-makers, links, tags/categories, upvote/engagement scores, reviews, comments, and maker launch schedule.
- **Categories**:
  - Path format: `/categories/{slug}` (e.g., `/categories/ai-code-editors`)
  - All categories page can be found at `/categories`
- **Makers / Profiles**:
  - Path format: `/profile?id={user_id}`
  - Includes user stats, followers, following, websites/social links, maker history, and hexagon badges.
- **Discussions & Threads**:
  - Path format: `/threads/{id}`
- **Stories**:
  - Path format: `/stories/{id}`
- **Launch Insights**:
  - Path format: `/launch-insights`

## Community & Roles
- **Makers**: Creators and developers of products.
- **Hunters**: Users who submit/discover products.
- **Community Members**: Engage through voting, comments, reviews, and threads.
